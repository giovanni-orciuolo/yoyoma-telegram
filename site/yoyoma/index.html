YoYo-Ma

A Telegram bot for group chats. It transcribes voice messages, finds songs from half-remembered lyrics and answers a handful of silly commands.

Open in Telegram Privacy policy

Voice messages

When someone sends a voice message, an audio file or a video message, the bot writes out what was said and replies with the text. Nobody has to listen to a three-minute voice message on the bus again. A group admin can turn this off with /config.

Marco 0:07
YoYo-Ma "Guys I am outside, who has the keys?"
Giulia /lyrics never gonna give you up
YoYo-Ma Rick Astley - Never Gonna Give You Up

Commands

  • /lyrics

    Finds a song on Genius from a piece of its lyrics. Send it alone and the bot asks you for the lyrics.

  • /tiktok/fbvideo

    Downloads a TikTok or Facebook video from its link and posts it in the chat, up to 50 MB.

  • /rcg

    Makes a random Cyanide and Happiness comic from three random panels.

  • /scp

    Links an SCP Foundation entry. Give it a number, or leave it empty for a random one.

  • /tesseract

    Reads the text in the next photo you send.

  • /mcstatus

    Shows whether a Minecraft server is online and who is playing, for example /mcstatus mc.hypixel.net.

  • /dadotext

    Writes your text on the dado video.

  • /cah

    Draws a random Cards Against Humanity combination.

  • /coin

    Flips a coin.

  • /ratecock

    Rates whoever you mention. Not for children.

  • /stickerid

    Tells you the ID of the next sticker you send.

  • /config

    Turns voice transcription and the sticker replies on or off, and switches between English and Italian. In a group, only admins can use it.

The name

Yo-Yo Ma is the eager, drooling Stand from JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Stone Ocean that follows its target around and helps with anything. This bot does the same, minus the drool. It is open source on GitHub.
